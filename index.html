About this mandala

This mandala is a learning tool for prenatal and perinatal educators. It gathers the healing modalities from Module 12 of APPPAH's Prenatal and Perinatal Educator program, Resolving and Healing, and shows what each one is, who it serves, when in life it fits, what a person can explore on their own, and when to refer to a trained practitioner.

The educator's part

An educator describes and refers. This mandala does not teach anyone to practice these modalities. Each one requires its own training outside this program, and some also require a license. Each card names who can practice it.

Finding a practitioner

Each card lists where to look for that modality. Two general directories can help with any of them:

  • APPPAH Practitioner Directory (birthpsychology.com)
  • Postpartum Support International provider directory (postpartum.net)

Links marked not verified or from module notes come from the course materials and have not yet been checked.

How it was made

Every card comes from one reference spreadsheet, built from the Module 12 readings and videos. The evidence levels are kept as they appear there: research-based, some research, case reports, and practice-based.
